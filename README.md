### **Information**
My Favorite movie is **Doctor Strange**. It is a Marvel movie that released on November 4, 2016 in the United States, directed by Scott Derrickson and starring Benedict Cumberbatch as Stephen Strange. 

### **About the Movie**
Dr. Stephen Strange, who seeks healing after a career-ending accident and discovers the mystic arts at Kamar-Taj. After losing the use of his hands, Dr. Stephen Strange searches for a cure and discovers the Ancient One and the Masters of the Mystic Arts. He learns to wield magical powers to protect Earth from mystical threats, ultimately confronting the villain Kaecilius and the cosmic entity Dormammu.

### **Why I like the Movie**
1. The charisma, and a mix of humor and gravitas to Stephen Strange, making the character relatable and magnetic. His portrayal balances the arrogant, sarcastic “Stephen” with the disciplined, mystical “Doctor Strange,” earning praise for emotional range and screen presence.
2. The film’s CGI and practical effects are widely regarded as some of the most imaginative in the MCU, from the kaleidoscopic “Time Stone” sequences to the surreal Dormammu battle. These visuals push the boundaries of superhero cinema, making the fantasy feel both real and otherworldly.
3. The fight scenes are not just action-packed but visually inventive — such as the Ancient One’s battle on a moving building or Strange’s duel with Dormammu. These sequences blend spectacle with narrative purpose, making them memorable.
